What is FindRun

A collection you can only build by moving.

FindRun is a fitness game where the rewards are real collectible cards. You don't buy packs — you earn them by running to where they surface. Every card you own is proof you were there.

Cards worth chasing

Every drop is a gem, an artifact never catalogued before, or a fact card. Some are common. A few are one in a thousand.

Found in real zones

Cards appear in zones seeded by real runs — including routes other people have already covered near you. Get within reach and it's yours.

Fitness that counts

Distance, pace, streak and cards-per-mile — tracked on every hunt. The fitter you get, the more ground you cover, the more you find.

How you find a card

Four steps. One good run.

Open the map

Active zones glow near you — each one holds cards waiting to be reached.

Run to the zone

Pick a card, follow your route, and close the distance on foot. Your run is tracked the whole way.

The card reveals

Get within 200 ft and the card flips — gem, artifact or fact. First one there takes it.

Keep it forever

It lands in your collection with the zone, date and rarity stamped on it. Yours, permanently.

Zones

Cards live where runners have been.

A zone is a patch of the real map that's been activated by a run — yours, or someone else's who came through before you. Active zones are where cards surface, so the more your city runs, the more there is to find.

Reach an active zone on foot and its card is in range. No zone, no card — which means every collection is a map of the ground you've actually covered.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I have to run to get cards?
Yes — that's the whole point. Cards surface in zones out in the world, and the only way to collect one is to reach it on foot. Walk it if you like; the app tracks the distance either way.
What's a zone?
A zone is a patch of the real map that's been activated by a run — yours or another runner's who passed through before you. Active zones are where cards appear, so busier areas have more to find.
What's the difference between gems, artifacts and facts?
Gems are valuable collectible stones with a rarity and a trade value. Artifacts are one-of-a-kind discoveries — the first person to find one is credited on the card forever. Facts are real, verifiable snippets about your body and your city that you collect into sets.
Is it free?
FindRun is free to play during the beta. There are no packs to buy — the only way to get a card is to go find it.
Does it actually track my fitness?
It does. Distance, pace, day streak and cards-per-mile are logged on every run, so it works as a genuine running tracker even on the days the drops are quiet.
When can I play?
We're rolling out on iPhone (iOS 17+) through TestFlight. Drop your email below and we'll send an invite as spots open in your city.
