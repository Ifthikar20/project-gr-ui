One-liner

RunnerCard turns walking into a collection. Every day, large zones land on parks and trails near you; walk a mile inside one and a collectible Runner Card mints — stamped with the walk that earned it.

Boilerplate

RunnerCard is a collectible walking game for iPhone. Instead of rewarding a run after the fact, it gives you a reason to get out the door: large daily zones anchored on public parks and walkable trails — never private or government land — where covering a GPS-verified mile mints a trading-style card: gems, gear, creatures, artifacts and facts across five rarities at published odds, each stamped with the distance, steps and date that earned it. The app quietly tracks distance, pace and streaks, synced with Apple Health, and runner data is stored on-device first.

Facts

  • Platform: iPhone, iOS 17+ (iPhone-only for now)
  • Status: TestFlight beta
  • Price: free to play — cards have no monetary value
  • Map data: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL)
  • Cards: 5 types (Gem, Gear, Creature, Artifact, Fact) × 5 rarities (Common → Legendary, 1 in 900)

Brand

A light, bevel-soft system: ink type on a cool paper ground, volt green kept for small accents, and one edge color per card rarity. Grays are opacity steps of ink — never another hue.
