Terms & fair play

Last updated August 2026 · applies to the TestFlight beta

It's a beta

RunnerCard is pre-release software. Zones rotate daily by design, mint rules may change, and occasionally we'll reset a leaderboard while we tune the economy. We'll always say so in the changelog.

One runner, one account

Keep it to one account per person. Usernames that impersonate other people get renamed.

Fair play

The whole game rests on claims being real. Every run is verified server-side — pace bounds, teleport detection, route adherence, and accuracy gating. Spoofed or simulated GPS voids the claims involved, and repeat offenders lose the account. If you found a clever exploit, we'd honestly love to hear about it instead: hey@runnercard.app.

Cards aren't money

Runner Cards, gems, XP, streaks and crowns have no monetary value, can't be bought, sold or transferred, and may be rebalanced during the beta. The fun is the point.

Routes you publish

Routes are public the moment you publish them. Keep them on walkable, legal, public ground — placement is checked automatically, but use judgement too. Every route has a report option; reported routes get reviewed and may be archived.

Run safe

Heads up, headphones low, obey the road. RunnerCard is designed to be played without looking at the screen — let the haptics do the work. You're responsible for moving safely; never let a mile pull you into traffic or anywhere you shouldn't be.

Liability, briefly

RunnerCard is provided as-is during the beta, without warranties. To the extent the law allows, we're not liable for indirect damages arising from use of the app.
