Changelog

TestFlight beta · iPhone, iOS 17+ · newest first

  1. 0.9.4 build 241 August 2, 2026
    • Stocking v2: every map open now fills the area on a per-mile contract — popular routes first, then walkable streets straight from OpenStreetMap.
    • Overpass mirror flapping fixed with a proper circuit breaker; a region losing one mirror no longer stalls gem spawns.
    • diagnose now names the exact broken link in the gem chain around any coordinate.
  2. 0.9.3 build 228 July 11, 2026
    • New run screen: chase camera starts at your position and rotates to your live direction of travel, with a breadcrumb trail of every step.
    • Crash-safe run buffer — force-quit or dead battery mid-run, and the run resumes where it stopped.
    • Rarity-scaled haptics: a Quartz is a single thump, an Ember is an escalating triple burst.
  3. 0.9.2 build 214 June 6, 2026
    • Walkability enforcement everywhere: drops on highways or private land are rejected outright, and route paths snap to real walking directions at creation.
    • Never-collected gems now flag themselves for review — placement trust, layered.
  4. 0.9.1 build 202 May 9, 2026
    • Claims are now settled atomically — one shared world, exactly one winner per gem, even in a photo finish.
    • Every claim attempt (winners and losers) lands in the race audit with outcome and closest distance.
    • GPS fixes worse than 50 m accuracy can no longer prove presence.
  5. 0.9.0 build 190 April 3, 2026
    • TestFlight opens 🎉 — the full loop is live: spawn → map → collect → settle → audit.
    • Earn-by-running wallet: lifetime kilometers mint droppable gems at per-tier thresholds.
    • Ember (Legendary) is system-seeded only — nobody farms their own crowns.
  6. 0.8.6 build 171 March 2, 2026
    • Suggested routes are now seeded from real street geometry — the geometric-circle routes are gone for good.
    • Destination mode: tap a point, get a snapped walking path from where you stand.
