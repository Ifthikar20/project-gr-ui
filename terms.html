Terms & fair play

Last updated August 2026 · applies to the TestFlight beta

It's a beta

GemRun is pre-release software. Gems may move, respawn rules may change, and occasionally we'll reset a leaderboard while we tune the economy. We'll always say so in the changelog.

One runner, one account

Keep it to one account per person. Usernames that impersonate other people get renamed.

Fair play

The whole game rests on claims being real. Every run is verified server-side — pace bounds, teleport detection, route adherence, and accuracy gating. Spoofed or simulated GPS voids the claims involved, and repeat offenders lose the account. If you found a clever exploit, we'd honestly love to hear about it instead: hello@gemrun.app.

Gems aren't money

Gems, XP, streaks and crowns have no monetary value, can't be bought, sold or transferred, and may be rebalanced during the beta. The fun is the point.

Routes you publish

Routes are public the moment you publish them. Keep them on walkable, legal, public ground — placement is checked automatically, but use judgement too. Every route has a report option; reported routes get reviewed and may be archived.

Run safe

Heads up, headphones low, obey the road. GemRun is designed to be played without looking at the screen — let the haptics do the work. You're responsible for running safely; don't chase an Ember into traffic.

Liability, briefly

GemRun is provided as-is during the beta, without warranties. To the extent the law allows, we're not liable for indirect damages arising from use of the app.
