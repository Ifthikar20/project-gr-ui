One-liner

GemRun turns any running route into a treasure hunt. The system drops gems on real streets; run within 100 ft to claim them — one shared world, first one there takes it.

Boilerplate

GemRun is a gamified running experience for iPhone. Instead of rewarding a run after the fact, it gives you a reason to get out the door: virtual gems placed on real, walkable streets, verified by GPS and claimed first-come-first-served. Runners collect, build streaks, design routes with gem placement as level design, and race friends on per-route leaderboards — while the app quietly tracks distance, splits, elevation and calories, synced with Apple Health. Runner data is stored on-device first; only claim-verification tracks and public leaderboard entries ever reach the server.

Facts

  • Platform: iPhone, iOS 17+ (iPhone-only for now)
  • Status: TestFlight beta
  • Price: free to play — gems have no monetary value
  • Map data: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL)
